Quicksy is a spin-off of the popular XMPP client Monal with automatic contact discovery.

You sign up using your phone number, and Quicksy will automatically — based on the phone numbers in your address book — suggest possible contacts to you. Under the hood, Quicksy is a full-fledged XMPP client that allows you to communicate with any user on any publicly federating server. Likewise, Quicksy users can be contacted from the outside simply by adding [+phonenumber@quicksy.im](mailto:+phonenumber@quicksy.im) to your contact list.

Apart from contact synchronisation, the user interface is deliberately kept as close to Monal as possible. This allows users to eventually migrate from Quicksy to Monal without having to relearn how the app works.
